What I think about design

We're all trying to get stuff done, progress, experience. The way things are designed either help us or hinder us in that journey. And (un)fortunately, we get to trial and error what works best for experiencer. Everything we do is designed by nature, crafted by humans, and experienced by everyone.

Work style

As a Product Designer and having worked mostly in the startup setting, I’m used to doing everything from research planning, to conducting moderated and unmoderated testing, to putting the final touches and documentation to designs. Discover, define, and execute.

I’ve worked on many products, including financial tooling and reporting at Teamshares, health benefits and internal tools with Justworks, consumer lending with Petal.
